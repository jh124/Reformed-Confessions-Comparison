# A Topical Harmony of Nine Reformation Confessions — Reorganization Blueprint

This is the **structural plan** for the finished document. It assigns every chapter, article, and
catechism question of all nine confessions to a topic section. Within each section, the portions
are listed in the **publishing-date order** below. The ranges are arranged to *partition* each
document, so that when the verbatim text is filled in, **every word of every confession is used at
least once**. Some portions are deliberately listed under more than one topic (this is allowed).

## Publishing dates (the ordering used inside every section)

1. **AC** — Augsburg Confession — 1530
2. **Scots** — Scots Confession — 1560
3. **BC** — Belgic Confession — 1561
4. **HC** — Heidelberg Catechism — 1563
5. **2HC** — Second Helvetic Confession — 1566 *(composed 1561–62; published 1566)*
6. **39A** — Thirty-Nine Articles — 1571 *(doctrinal substance fixed 1563; final 39-article form 1571)*
7. **Dort** — Canons of Dort — 1619
8. **WCF** — Westminster Confession of Faith (original British text) — 1646
9. **WLC** — Westminster Larger Catechism — 1647

> Note on one ordering judgment call: the Thirty-Nine Articles vs. the Second Helvetic. The Articles'
> doctrine is essentially 1563, but the final *thirty-nine*-article form is 1571. I've sequenced them
> at **1571**, after the Second Helvetic. Say the word and I'll flip them to 1563.
